MRA-PRD-202Delivery planRev 2.0 · 7 Nov 2026Supersedes MRA-PRD-002

Prioritised delivery plan: test first, then build where the hours are

Six weeks of tests decide whether to build. If they pass, a 26-week pilot builds client intake and the contradiction check first, then drafting, then measures the result on two live 510(k)s. Research tools, self-serve and the Lead Finder wait until the pilot proves value.

Phase V9 Nov – 18 Dec 2026. No product build; scripts and human checking only.
Pilot26 weeks from Gate V. Week 1 is assumed to be 4 Jan 2027, subject to funding.
BudgetWithin the $650K build line. Shortfall of 4–16 engineer-weeks before CTO sizing (§8).
RequirementsMRA-PRD-201 (FR-14) and the kept v1.0 items

v1.0's rule was "hours first". It was the right rule applied to an unmeasured task list. v2.0 applies it to the hours we measured: 60 of 98 on client material, 10 of 98 on FDA research. Nothing is built yet, so changing the order costs a planning decision rather than rework.

10Fallbacks, in order

  1. (a) Manufacturers' RA teams. Sell the same workflow to manufacturers that file repeatedly (about 1,869 companies with 2–4 clearances and 717 with 5 or more, 2021–2025). Validate with one RA/QA lead first.
  2. (b) Organiser only. Sell intake, sorting and the contradiction check at a lower price, without drafting.
  3. (c) Stop. If neither passes, stop the build and keep the cash.
