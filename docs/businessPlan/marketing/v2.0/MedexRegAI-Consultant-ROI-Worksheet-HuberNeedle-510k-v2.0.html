What the consultant's data tells us

  1. Smaller scope. Five tasks (test plan, biocompatibility plan, lab coordination, test-report review, risk file) are "usually done by the manufacturer (>90%)". The consultant's job is strategy, predicates, the SE argument and the eSTAR.
  2. General AI barely helps today. The consultant saves 6 hours out of 104 (6%), all of it in research: predicate search, the SE matrix and the research library. Drafting sections took the same time with or without AI.
  3. Research is faster than we assumed. Predicate search took 4 h, not 16. Verifying predicates took 2 h, not 8.
  4. The SE argument and paperwork take longer. The SE matrix (12 h vs 10), device description (8 vs 6) and admin documents (8 vs 6) were under-estimated.
  5. Where the value now is. MedexRegAI saves time on the tasks where the consultant already sees AI help, and those are its differentiated features: the SE matrix, research library, eSTAR assembly, claim-evidence QC and review tracking.

3. Who else does the work: manufacturer, test labs and suppliers

A 510(k) is a team effort. The consultant confirmed that the manufacturer owns the test strategy and risk file. Labs and suppliers produce the evidence. MedexRegAI gives the client's team free seats in the consultant's workspace, so the same predicate precedent, test plan and evidence record serve everyone. That is extra value for the project that a consultant-only ROI leaves out.

The consultant left the pump section blank, so the pump figures are still model v1: 144.5 hours saved, 23.1x. Given what the Huber data showed, treat that number as too high.

8. Method

Consultant data. One practising 510(k) consultant filled in their actual hours, with and without the AI tools they use, for the 26 Huber-needle tasks in the validation workbook (received 25 Oct 2026). Their answers are shown exactly as given. They have not yet given permission to be named, so this page does not identify them.

v2 MedexRegAI estimate. For the 20 tasks the consultant does: on research and verification tasks (our categories M and V), the consultant's hours with AI × the v1 ratio of MedexRegAI to general-AI hours. On drafting and human-only tasks (categories A and H), it equals the consultant's hours with AI, so no extra credit. MedexRegAI is pre-launch, so these are still targets to measure in the 12-week pilot.

Manufacturer hours are model v1 figures for the tasks the consultant assigned to the manufacturer. Test labs and suppliers are shown by role only; no hours or savings are claimed for them.
