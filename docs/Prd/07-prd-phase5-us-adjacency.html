1Purpose

After clearance, a consultancy's client keeps changing the device: labeling, materials, suppliers, software. Each change raises the most frequent recurring question in device regulation: does this need a new 510(k) or a letter to file? Phase 5 answers it with the same evidence base, and adds drafting and response help where the pilot showed the hours still sit.

6Lead Finder (M15)

  • Consultant channel subscription with filters (product code, panel, class, geography, engagement size) and lead credits (BP §7).
  • Consequence-tiered lead pricing; 72-hour exclusivity; automatic refund for unreachable leads.
  • Double opt-in from the free-tier user; nothing leaves the tenant without it (L-2).
  • Hard separation: no revenue signal reaches the determination model or its prompts (BP standing gate).
  • For the pilot consultancy: they become a supply-side partner, and their free client workspaces count toward consultant-sourced accounts.

7Other adjacency scope

  • Onboarding for non-US applicants filing into the US: localised interface and partner distribution (BP E-2).
  • Clinical trial and IDE data linkage from ClinicalTrials.gov (BP gap closing, M13–16).
  • Registration change intelligence from establishment data, deliberately not registration management (BP).

8Exit

  • Change assessment in use at ≥ 10 accounts with cleared devices.
  • Response drafts accepted with light edits ≥ 60% of the time.
  • Lead Finder live with ≥ 14 consultant channels (conservative case, BP §16).
  • Series A readiness at M18 (BP).
