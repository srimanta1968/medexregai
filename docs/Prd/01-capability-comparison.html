1Summary

What Veeva is

A system of record for executing regulated work

Veeva unifies documents, data and workflow for pharma regulatory, quality, safety, clinical and commercial teams. It is strongest after a strategy is decided: planning, authoring, publishing, filing, archiving and updating registrations.

What we are

Intelligence on the public FDA record

MedexRegAI answers the questions that come first: what the device is, which pathway, which predicates, what testing, what it will cost. Veeva's videos show none of that research layer.

What the pilot needs

Our intelligence plus a light workspace

The consultancy's 50% saving needs our research engine plus a light version of five Veeva patterns: content plan, continuous checks, FDA question handling, version history and a chronology record.

Bottom line. Don't compete with Veeva as a RIM system of record; the business plan rules that out, and it isn't where the consultant's hours go. Compete on research and decision support, which Veeva doesn't show. Adopt Veeva's best workflow ideas only as light registers that sit next to the customer's existing document storage.

3Veeva's system map, as the videos show it

Veeva's main idea is one platform holding documents and data together, so a change made once (a new manufacturer, a shelf-life extension, an approval) flows into registrations, submissions, safety and quality without re-keying. Most of its AI announcements in 2026 are agents working on data already inside the customer's Vault.

10Veeva patterns to adopt, and how far

Adopt

The plan is the hub

One screen holds the eSTAR structure on the left and owners, dates, evidence and status on the right (V-S2).

Adopt

Check continuously, not at the end

Re-run completeness and precedent gap rules every time evidence changes (V-P1).

Adopt

Letters become records

An FDA letter becomes structured deficiencies linked to responses and evidence (V-H1–H4).

Adopt

Chronology as an export

One date-ordered record of submissions, questions and decisions (V-A2).

Adopt, later

Change → impact assessment

The pattern behind 510(k) change assessment (V-R2, V-R3), in US adjacency.

Avoid

Document storage and publishing

Co-authoring, e-signature, eCTD publishing and gateway filing (V-S4, V-P2–P4) make us a system of record.
