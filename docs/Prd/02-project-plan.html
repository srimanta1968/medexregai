Phased project plan: pilot first, platform second

How MedexRegAI is built in five phases so that one 510(k) consultancy gets a measured 50% effort reduction within six months, and the same work becomes the month-6 baseline the business plan commits to.

AnchorsMRA-BIZ-001 r6 milestones: baseline M6, GA M12, Lead Finder M15, Series A M18
BudgetWithin the $650K build line of MRA-PLN-001 r2.0, plus about $28K proposed (§9)
Pilot26 weeks, 2 live 510(k)s, stop/go gate at week 12
RequirementsPRD 1–5 in this set; new group FR-13 "Submission Workspace"

The pilot is not a separate project. Every pilot feature is either baseline scope the business plan already funds (predicates, research, citations, workspaces) or Release 1 scope pulled forward (content checklist, gap audit). The only new scope is the light Submission Workspace (FR-13). It is what lets a consultancy reach 50%, and it gives the $500/month retainer something to do between projects.

2Planning rules

  1. Hours first. Build in the order the consultant's hours are spent: research before workflow.
  2. Prove before widening. No Submission Workspace build until week-12 evidence shows the research core saves time.
  3. Registers, not repositories. We store links, hashes, statuses and relationships. Files stay in the customer's SharePoint or Drive. We never file with FDA.
  4. One codebase. The pilot runs on the production architecture, in its own tenant. No throwaway prototype.
  5. Narrow data, full pipeline. Start with the customer's product codes through the full ingestion pipeline, then widen to the whole corpus for M6.
  6. Citations and snapshots from day one. They are architectural (FM-2 in the business plan) and can't be added later.

7Measuring the 50% reduction
