PRD 2: Submission Workspace

A light workspace that turns research into a ready 510(k): the eSTAR plan, a continuous gap check, a claim–evidence matrix, FDA question handling and an auditable record of what was submitted. It adapts Veeva's best RIM patterns without becoming a document system.

Pilot areas3 Plan · 4 Claims and evidence · 5 FDA questions and versions · 6 Audit record
New requirementsFR-13.1 – FR-13.9 · FR-5.1–5.3 pulled forward
Starts only ifGate G1 passed at week 12
Veeva patternsV-S2, V-S3, V-P1, V-P4, V-H1–H4, V-A1, V-A2, V-AI5, V-Q3

1Purpose and outcome

≥ 50%less time on the eSTAR checklist
≥ 40%less time cross-checking claims
≥ 40%less time on AI-letter response packages
≥ 70%less time reconstructing the record

The consultant plans each eSTAR section, sees which evidence covers it, and gets warned early about gaps that comparable submissions show FDA asks about. When FDA sends an AI request, the letter becomes a list of tracked deficiencies with suggested evidence and similar past deficiencies. At any time the consultant can export a dated record of what was submitted, what FDA asked and what changed.

2Scope rule: registers, not repositories

We store references (link, file hash, version label, author, date), statuses, relationships and extracted structure. The files stay in the customer's SharePoint, Google Drive or eQMS. We don't provide check-in/out, e-signatures, Part 11 document control, eSTAR form filling or FDA transmission. This amends REQ §2 to read "a register of submission artefacts, not a document control system".

Confidentiality. AI letters are confidential client records. Cross-client similarity within a consultancy is off by default. When the owner turns it on, results show deficiency text only, never the other client's name or device, unless the user has access to that workspace.

11Exit gate G2 (week 22)

  • All six pilot areas in daily use on 2 live 510(k)s.
  • Gap check validated against the customer's past AI letters: ≥ 60% of past deficiencies would have been flagged as a finding.
  • Deficiency splitting ≥ 95% correct on pilot letters; correspondence type ≥ 95%.
  • Tenant isolation tests pass for similar-deficiency search.
  • No blocking defects open; exports open cleanly in Word and Excel.
