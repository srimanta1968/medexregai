PRD v2.0: from FDA research to client evidence

A rewrite of the PRD set after consultant feedback. We keep the FDA data layer, but the product now starts where consultants say their time goes: turning the files clients send into eSTAR-ready sections that a consultant reviews in Word.

SupersedesMRA-PRD-002 and the build order in PRD 1–5 (Rev 1.0, 8 Oct 2026). Absorbs the v4.0 PRD addendum (Rev 0.1).
StatusDraft for founder approval. Product build starts only after Gate V (18 Dec 2026).
CompanionBusiness plan r7 (MRA-BIZ-001 r7)
Evidence baseOne consultant's task hours (n = 1), one consultant email, and public FDA sources. Targets are proposed, not measured.

1Why there is a v2.0

60 of 98 hConsultant hours spent turning client material into the submission. v1.0 does not reach this until M13–22.
10 of 98 hFDA research, predicate search and verification. v1.0 builds this first.
18 → 34 hHours saved on a simple 510(k): v3.0 committed vs v2.0 target. Complex: 38 → 92 h. Targets only.
0.06x → 2.1xA firm's modelled first-year return (Team plan, half of freed hours monetised)

On 30 October 2026 a practising 510(k) consultant told us that peers doubted the product's value and its fit with their existing IT. Consultancies already run an eQMS (TrackWise or equivalent), accounting, onboarding, sales and data-exchange tools (Dropbox or equivalent). Any new tool adds training, validation and maintenance. In their view the real challenge is managing what clients send (test reports, clinical protocols, raw data, specifications and drawings, in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF and other formats) and turning it into submissions. So the platform as planned would be a nice-to-have.

Our own time data supports this. v1.0 kept the right rule (build in the order the hours are spent) but applied it to the wrong task list. v2.0 keeps the rule and changes the order.

4Status, and what is still unproven

Nothing in v2.0 has been validated yet. On 7 Nov 2026 we have one consultant's hours (Huber needle 510(k), 98 h with general AI) and one email. The peer workbook (Part A) is due back on 20 Nov. We have not yet measured how much of the 60 hours is finding, chasing and reconciling information and how much is expert judgement. If it is mostly judgement, drafting will not help much and the "nice-to-have" verdict stands. Gate V exists to answer that before we spend build money.

  • Every hour and ROI figure from v3.0/v4.0 is labelled committed, target or modelled. None is a measured product result, because no product exists.
  • Consultant #1's written permission to use their anonymised hours is still outstanding (owner: CEO).
  • Competitors' capabilities (Complizen, Assyro) are vendor claims that we have not verified.
