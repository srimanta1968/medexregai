PRD 3: Pilot proof and baseline launch

Measure the pilot result, widen the data from the pilot's product codes to the full US corpus, and open the product to the market on the business-plan terms: PR-1 and PR-2, card checkout, retainer and credits, free tier.

Business plan"Baseline live and self-serve open", M6
ProjectsPR-1 Pathway Determination · PR-2 Predicate and Comparative Study
GateG3: pilot ≥ 50% and REQ §15 baseline thresholds
NoteCommerce items may slip to M7 by founder decision if the pilot needs the capacity

1Purpose

Phase 3 does two jobs. It produces the pilot's measured result and the customer's go/no-go. It also turns the pilot build into the month-6 public baseline: the same features on the full corpus, plus the self-serve purchase path the business model depends on.

7Hardening

  • Load test at 10× pilot usage; NFR-1 latency targets met.
  • External penetration test scheduled; critical findings fixed before public launch.
  • Backups and restore drill; disaster-recovery runbook.
  • Status page and incident process.
  • SOC 2 readiness programme started (policies, access reviews, change management).

8Exit gate G3 (week 26, M6)

  • Pilot: scoped hours ≥ 50% lower on ≥ 2 live 510(k)s; no rise in AI deficiencies; RA lead signs the report.
  • REQ §15 baseline thresholds met: top-3 code ≥ 90%, pathway ≥ 95%, precision@5 ≥ 0.80, 100% citations, extraction F1 ≥ 0.90.
  • At least four design partners on real devices (BP baseline milestone); the pilot consultancy counts as one.
  • First projects sold without a human involved.
