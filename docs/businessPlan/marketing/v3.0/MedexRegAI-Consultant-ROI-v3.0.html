1. How we built these numbers

1 · Real hours

A practising 510(k) consultant re-timed all 26 tasks of a Huber-needle Traditional 510(k): hours without AI and with the AI tools they use today.

2 · Real scope

The manufacturer does the test plan, biocompatibility plan, lab coordination, test-report review and risk file. We left those out of the consultant's hours.

3 · Honest baseline

We compare against the consultant's hours with general AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini), not against working without AI.

4 · Credit only where earned

MedexRegAI gets credit only on research, verification, eSTAR planning, quality checks and review tracking. It gets no credit for meetings or for drafting sections from test reports.

  • Substantial equivalence: the SE table is pre-filled from the predicate's 510(k) summary, with every cell cited (10 → 6 h).
  • Predicates you can defend: live K-number, recall and MAUDE checks on each candidate. In a draft 510(k) we reviewed, both predicate K-numbers were invalid.

  • Research and review: a dated library of guidance and recognised standards, and an FDA-clock dashboard during review (12 → 7.5 h).

6. Value we do not count in the ROI

Your client's team
  • The manufacturer owns the test plan, biocompatibility plan, lab work and risk file.
  • Free client seats put them in your workspace, where they get the test plan derived from what cleared predicates tested, and complaint and recall themes as hazard inputs.
  • Test labs, the sterilizer and suppliers send their reports into one evidence record linked to each claim.
Fewer FDA surprises
  • FY2025 510(k)s averaged 1.69 review cycles and 66 days on hold.
  • 7.2% failed eSTAR technical screening on the first try.
  • 8.3% ended NSE or deleted, which means a new 510(k) and a new $28,653 fee.
Defensible work
  • Every predicate, standard and recall carries its FDA source and a dated snapshot.
  • An immutable submission record and chronology for every project.
  • MedexRegAI never files and never signs. You stay in charge.

8. The fine print

Consultant data. One practising 510(k) consultant supplied their actual hours for the Huber-needle project (October 2026). Their hours are used as given. We are collecting more consultants' data and will publish averages and ranges.

Complex project. The infusion-pump hours are calibrated, not measured. Each pump task uses the ratio that consultant showed on the matching Huber task. Technical-documentation sections keep our model's hours, because the consultant's Huber section hours matched our model. The Pre-Sub uses the consultant's overall ratio. Answering FDA's Additional Information letter is left out of both projects until we confirm who does it.

MedexRegAI hours. MedexRegAI is launching in 2026–27. Savings are product targets, reviewed task by task against the consultant's real hours and conservatively reduced where needed (for example, eSTAR assembly is cut from a 50% to a 25% saving). They will be measured in a 12-week pilot.

Prices are the MedexRegAI published price ladder; cost per project = plan price ÷ projects included. FDA figures come from openFDA (data updated 12 Oct 2026) and FDA's MDUFA V performance report (26 Aug 2026). Not regulatory advice.
